**Entry name:** The hardest work I ever did was nothing
**Bounty:** the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927 — "The poem about stopping" ($35, top 20 share the prize)
**Entrant:** Mahardika (@dika-david)

A short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay. One hand held above
the switch, unmoving, while the machine hums go, go, go — and the choice not
to pull it. The insight: agency is not only the step you take, it is the step
you refuse, and the room it leaves.
